I teach AI and deep learning with a first-principles approach: connect the mathematics to tensor shapes, implementation details, training behavior, and experiments.

Teaching philosophy

  • Understand the idea before writing the code
  • Build architectures component by component
  • Explain dimensions, equations, and training decisions
  • Learn through experiments, not black-box shortcuts
